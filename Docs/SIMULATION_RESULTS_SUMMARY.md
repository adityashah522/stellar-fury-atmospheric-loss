**Assumptions**:
- Constant escape rate: 2.0 kg/s
- Time period: 1,000,000 years = 3.16 × 10¹³ seconds
- Steady-state outflow conditions

**Results**:
- **Total Mass Loss**: **63.1 Gigatonnes (Gt)**
  - = 6.31 × 10¹³ kg

**Fractional Loss**:
- Earth's total atmospheric mass: 5.15 × 10¹⁸ kg
- **Fraction lost in 1 Myr**: **0.00123%**

**Interpretation**:
- Extremely small fraction of total atmosphere
- Earth's atmosphere is stable over geological timescales
- Actual rates vary with:
  - Solar activity (11-year cycle)
  - Geomagnetic storm frequency  
  - Long-term solar evolution
  - Atmospheric composition changes

## Software & Methods

**SWMF Version**: 2025-11-14 (modified master)

- Mars: `Config.pl -e=MhdMars -u=Mars`
